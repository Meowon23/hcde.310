**Who could this data help, and with what?** (2–3 ideas; name real people and a real need, e.g. "first-year students who want to ...")

1.  learners who want books in the language they’re studying, on topics they care about.
2. students writing a paper who want to find books by suject , authors, first publication year, and a cover to recognize the book. The subject and search
3. Readers who can’t afford books, such as students or people on tight budget, who want to know which titles can be borrowed or read online.

**One risk** (missing data, bias, privacy, or reliability): Records are community maintained, so some books have missing or inconsistent fields,, also many lack cover images

**Who could this data help, and with what?** (2–3 ideas)

1. Music teacher/parents building listening list, using release dates and artist info to order it.
2. Fans who want to go deeper on one artist, such as who they’ve collaborated with
3. People who want to support local music, maybe someone in city looking for artists from their area.
